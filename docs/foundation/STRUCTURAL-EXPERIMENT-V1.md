# Structural experiment v1: frozen fitting protocol

Frozen before fit scoring, 2026-10-04. Internal-only visual experiment, not installation truth. No evaluation, fixture source, fixture dimensions, fixture cameras, fourth image, held-out annotations or benchmark results may be read by this worker. The only supplied metric quantity is the opening mouth width of 1.20 m. Neural depth, camera points and intrinsics are ignored in all lanes.

## Inputs and lanes

`python workers/structure/fit.py --input .image-blaster/structure-v1/input --annotations annotations.json --lane assisted --output .image-blaster/structure-v1/assisted`

Allowlisted input files: `view-01.png`, `view-02.png`, `view-03.png`, `hints.npz`, `manifest.json` `constraint.json`, and the explicitly selected annotation basename. Image IDs are `view01`, `view02`, `view03`. NPZ normals are [3,H,W,3] OpenCV camera-axis normals with masks [3,H,W]; arrays must already be sliced to these three independent unconditioned views. The immutable input manifest retains neural identity and upstream artifact hashes. Annotations are `{schemaVersion:1,views:[{id,points:{name:[u,v]}}]}` in original image pixels. Max 24 visible points per view, 72 total. Omit occluded or ambiguous points, never infer hidden ones. Opening bottom endpoints are required in each participating view; conflicting anchors fail closed.

Names: opening.bottomLeft/bottomRight/topLeft/topRight; hearth.topBackLeft/topBackRight/topFrontLeft/topFrontRight/bottomFrontLeft/bottomFrontRight; mantel.bottomBackLeft/bottomBackRight/bottomFrontLeft/bottomFrontRight/topFrontLeft/topFrontRight; room.backFloorLeft/backFloorRight/backCeilingLeft/backCeilingRight/frontFloorLeft/frontFloorRight/frontCeilingLeft/frontCeilingRight. `back` room intersections mean the fireplace wall, not an unseen wall behind the observer.

The physically separate `constraint.json` is `{"kind":"opening-mouth-width","width_m":1.20}`. It is the sole effective metric input; normalized model bounds scale with this width. The immutable neural manifest's historical constraint field is not used. Automatic requires the physically separate `endpoints.json` and receives only mouth bottom endpoints, RGB and independent normals. It attempts Canny/Hough matching of a supported top edge and reports candidate evidence and failed selections. Assisted receives the same RGB/normals plus the visible corner budget. Ablation receives identical assisted points and RGB but never reads neural arrays or the neural manifest. All lanes use the identical neutral camera initialization and focal bounds; none initializes from neural intrinsics or supplied poses.

## Frozen algorithm and hypotheses

Bounded robust multiview nonlinear least squares jointly fits explicitly rectangular, planar Manhattan geometry and unknown per-view cameras. World coordinates are right-handed, Y-up, metres. The mouth bottom midpoint defines [0,0,0], its left/right endpoints [-0.6,0,0]/[0.6,0,0], and its wall is z=0; the visible room extends along +z. This fixes the coordinate/scale gauge and is not an externally registered pose. Opening height, hearth/mantel lateral centers, widths, levels, thicknesses, front/back offsets, and room bounds are independently fitted only when their named points are observed. No cavity/recess depth is inferred or exported. Only annotated/detected points and complete supported visible quadrilateral faces are exported, never an invented closed box.

Principal point is fixed at the image center, square pixels, zero distortion. Per-view focal is unknown, initialized to max(image dimensions), bounded to [0.35,3.0] times that dimension. Camera position bounds are x [-10,10], y [-5,8], z [0.3,15] m; rotation delta about a front-facing camera is bounded to +/-0.9 radians per rotation-vector component. Finite bounds are modeling assumptions, not observations. Plane extent bounds are recorded in diagnostics. Robust loss is soft_l1, scale 1; pixel sigma 2 px, axial normal sigma sin(10 degrees), normal weight 0.5. Normals constrain orientation via nearest Manhattan axis, not offsets or depth; sign is explicitly ambiguous. Dominant clusters use deterministic 8-pixel sampling and angular suppression. Neural depth terms are absent.

Solver: scipy least_squares, max_nfev=120 per run, three deterministic starts (seed 1705), two +/-1 px annotation perturbation refits (seeds 1706,1707), and two width-only refits at 0.99 and 1.01 times the supplied width with identically normalized bounds. Parameters use explicit finite bounds. Selection uses minimum robust objective on fitting inputs only. Data Jacobian rank uses relative singular threshold 1e-6; bound contact is within 1e-3 of parameter range. Null directions, inadequate row count, nonconvergence, or bound contact mean underconstrained. Linearized covariance is conditional on the model and noise, never certainty; full-rank finite estimates do not establish truth. Perturbation ranges and all multi-start objective/parameter outcomes are retained.

## Metrics and gates frozen before scoring

Training diagnostics: per-view reprojection RMSE/p95 in original pixels, axial normal angular error, robust objective, convergence, Jacobian singular spectrum/rank/nullity, bound contacts, and parameter ranges over starts and perturbations. A training fit passes its provisional numerical gate only with full column rank, no bound contacts, solver convergence, positive camera depths, reprojection RMSE <=3 px and p95 <=6 px. This gate is necessary but insufficient; independent held-out scoring remains separate.

Evaluation contract for the parent: report aligned structural point/plane extent errors, wall/floor angular and offset errors, hearth/mantel front offsets and dimensions, mouth width/height and mouth-plane error, plus held-out fourth-view silhouette/edge reprojection from an independently estimated image-only camera. Fourth-view camera fitting must not use held-out corner outcomes being scored. Report unavailable measures as unknown. Acceptance gates fixed here: <=10 cm median and <=20 cm p95 visible point distance, <=5 degrees plane angle, <=10 cm visible plane offset, <=10% dimension error except anchored width (identity check only), <=8 px held-out reprojection median and <=16 px p95. Each applicable gate must pass; missing required geometry is a failed completeness gate. Compare all three lanes with the same alignment/evaluation policy. A supplied anchor is never counted as reconstruction success.

## Output, replay and isolation

`structure.json`: versioned structural evidence with coordinate convention, mouth anchor, observed point coordinates, visible planar surfaces and OpenCV estimated camera matrices. `geometry.obj`: only complete visible faces; may be empty if underconstrained. `predictions.json`: reprojected fitted points. `diagnostics.json`: settings, residuals, bounds, rank and conditional uncertainty, runs. `receipt.json`: all effective input/code/runtime hashes (including Pillow), validation of staged neural/image provenance against consumed bytes, neural identity for neural lanes, annotations and actual used points, artifact checksums. Cache identity excludes path/timestamps, includes effective lane/settings, image bytes, relevant annotation bytes, worker sources, numerical library versions and neural artifact/identity bytes when consumed. Replay verifies every output checksum; input mutation during fitting fails before acceptance. Existing nonmatching outputs are not overwritten.

A CPython audit hook permits only exact selected input files, worker source files, Python/runtime library trees and output writes. It denies other file opens, directory enumeration, subprocesses and networking after initialization. Audit receipts record denied read probes. This is an accidental-leakage guard around trusted code, not an OS sandbox against hostile native extensions. Analytical tests cover transforms, signs, scale/offset construction, rank deficiency, missing/conflicting endpoints, and forbidden reads. No paid execution, new dependencies, UI, fixture edits, evaluation reads or client integration.

### Evaluation clarification frozen before first scoring

Independent evaluator uses datum rigid alignment: declared +X mouth width, +Y up, +Z room and mouth bottom midpoint translation only, scale exactly 1. Reports unoriented plane angle with separate signed-normal consistency, signed centroid plane offset, bidirectional sampled finite-polygon distance, truth-visible coverage within 5 cm, missing and false surfaces, prior entity table including unsupported props, observed XYZ spans. Four oracle-camera projected boundary nearest distances and 3D-to-truth depth residual/coverage are diagnostic only; report fitting views 1-3 separately from withheld view4. Estimated-camera annotation reprojection is a fitting metric only. The width anchor is excluded from independent accuracy. Oracle camera diagnostics do not establish independently estimated novel-view cameras. Gates above apply where metrics match; unavailable independent-camera evidence remains unverified.


Final pre-score clarification: full fireplace-wall quads are intentionally omitted because a quad would fill the opening aperture. The opening mouth is a reference aperture surface in structural JSON, excluded from solid OBJ faces. Unobservable back/depth and floor/height decompositions remain numeric diagnostic candidates explicitly marked `identifiable:false` with nullspace loading; no canonical dimensions are asserted. Every exported face records the source view/point IDs and underconstrained/conditional uncertainty. Dominant-normal receipts retain sampled, accepted, excluded counts and selected axial clusters.

Independent evaluation uses a fixed 2.5 cm triangle lattice. All visible truth samples, including unsupported furniture, count toward 5 cm completeness. It reports nearest-proposal plane angle/offset separately from finite extent median/p95 and marks a truth face missing when no samples lie within 5 cm. Proposal-to-truth finite extent median/p95 and unsupported-visible fraction provide the reverse distance. False-visible fraction means depth disagreement greater than 5 cm after ignoring occluded proposal samples. Camera center, rotation and focal errors are scored only after fixed datum alignment, separately from geometry. Independent view4 camera acceptance is explicitly unavailable/false in this split; oracle-camera diagnostics cannot satisfy it. These metrics and gates were declared before first scoring.
